Riyaz Privacy Policy

 

Effective 4 November 2026 · App: Riyaz · Developer: Qustrium

 
    In short: Riyaz records your practice sessions and keeps the recordings safely stored on your own device. There are no user accounts, no advertisements, and no analytics tracking. We do not run any external servers that collect or view your recordings.  
 

1. What Riyaz does

 

Riyaz is a practice recorder designed for musicians. It records audio and video, displays a practice stopwatch, detects tempo (beats per minute) in real-time, and provides shake correction so you can easily review your practice takes.

 

2. Information the app uses

 
                                       
InformationWhyWhere it stays
Camera videoTo record your practice and show the live camera feedOn your device only
Microphone audioTo record your practice and to measure tempo and input sound levelsOn your device only
Tempo readings, marks & take namesTo generate your tempo graphs and remember your practice flagsCalculated locally and stored with the recording in your browser/app storage
Settings (target tempo, camera/mic selection, save preferences)To remember your app preferencesOn your device only
 
 

All tempo calculations are performed locally on your device. Your audio is never sent to external servers for analysis.

 

We do not collect your name, email address, physical location, contacts, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers.

 

3. Where your recordings are stored

 

Recordings are stored inside local app storage on your device. We have no remote access to your files. If you choose to download, share, or save a recording to your file manager, that file goes to the destination of your choice and is governed by that service's respective policies.

 

4. Permissions the app asks for

 
       
  • Camera and Microphone: Required to capture video and audio recordings. Riyaz utilizes them only while the app is active. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device Settings.
    •    
  • Vibration: Provides short haptic feedback when you mark a specific moment in your practice session.
    •    
  • Keep screen on (Wake Lock): Prevents the screen from dimming or sleeping while actively recording.
    •    
  • Network access: Used exclusively to load web components and execute optional user-initiated Google Drive uploads.
    •  
 

5. Data Sharing

 

We do not sell, rent, track, or share your personal information because we never collect or receive it in the first place. Any file sharing is strictly initiated by you (e.g., sharing a video take via your system share menu or exporting to a cloud folder).

 

6. Third-Party Services

 
       
  • Google Fonts: The app loads typography assets (IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif) via Google Fonts. When fetching fonts, your device communicates with Google's servers, exposing your IP address as with standard internet browsing. No audio, video, or personal details are transmitted. See Google's Privacy Policy.
    •    
  • Google Drive Upload (Optional): If you choose to enable automatic Google Drive backup in Settings, you will be prompted to sign in with your Google Account. Riyaz requests limited scope access (drive.file), granting access only to the files and "Riyaz" folder it creates itself. Uploads pass directly from your device to your personal Google Drive storage without routing through any intermediary servers of ours. You can revoke this access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
    •  
 

7. Keeping and deleting your data

 
       
  • Delete an individual practice take directly within the app using the Delete button under that take.
    •    
  • Clear all application data by clearing browser/app storage or uninstalling the app from your device.
    •    
  • Saved or uploaded copies in third-party services (like Google Drive) remain until you delete them from those respective locations.
    •  
 

8. Children's Privacy

 

Riyaz is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because all practice recordings, tempo data, and settings remain strictly stored on your local device and are never sent to our servers, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. Parents or guardians can review, manage, or delete any recordings directly on the device at any time.

 

9. Security

 

Your practice recordings are protected by your device's built-in OS security. We recommend keeping a strong device passcode or biometrics enabled. No electronic storage method is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

 

10. Changes to this policy

 

If app features change in a manner that affects user privacy, this policy document will be updated alongside the effective date listed above.
